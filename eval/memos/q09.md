# Which 5 customer states wait the longest on average for delivery, in days from purchase?

**Decision:** Identify customer states with the longest average delivery wait times to understand potential issues and improve service in those areas.
**Metric:** Average delivery wait time in days

## Answer

Customer states RR, AP, AM, AL, and PA have the longest average delivery wait times, with RR at 29.85 days, AP at 27.18 days, AM at 26.36 days, AL at 24.48 days, and PA at 23.71 days.

## Recommendation

Investigate the reasons for the longer delivery times in these states, such as increased demand, logistical challenges, or seller performance issues, and implement strategies to address them.

## Evidence

```sql
SELECT customer_state, AVG(delivery_days) AS avg_delivery_days FROM orders WHERE purchase_month BETWEEN '2017-01-01' AND '2018-08-01' AND delivery_days IS NOT NULL GROUP BY customer_state ORDER BY avg_delivery_days DESC LIMIT 5
```

| customer_state | avg_delivery_days |
|---|---|
| RR | 29.85 |
| AP | 27.18 |
| AM | 26.36 |
| AL | 24.48 |
| PA | 23.71 |

## Checks

- SQL passed the read-only checks and ran (5 rows, 1 attempt).
- All numbers in this memo were found in the query result.

## Caveats

The data does not provide information on the reasons behind the longer delivery times or the effectiveness of any interventions. Orders run from 2016-09-04 to 2018-10-17. 2016 has only a few hundred orders and the months after August 2018 are incomplete, so trends should use 2017-01 to 2018-08.
