# What is the average number of items per order, for orders with at least one item?

**Decision:** To understand the typical size of an order on the Olist marketplace, which can help in inventory planning and logistics.
**Metric:** Average number of items per order

## Answer

The average number of items per order is 1.14. This indicates that most orders consist of a single item.

## Recommendation

Given that most orders are for a single item, the company may want to consider optimizing for smaller, more frequent orders rather than larger bulk orders.

## Evidence

```sql
SELECT AVG(items) AS avg_items_per_order FROM orders WHERE items > 0
```

| avg_items_per_order |
|---|
| 1.14 |

## Checks

- SQL passed the read-only checks and ran (1 rows, 1 attempt).
- All numbers in this memo were found in the query result.

## Caveats

This average does not account for the distribution of item sizes or the potential impact of promotional sales that might lead to higher numbers of smaller items per order. Orders run from 2016-09-04 to 2018-10-17. 2016 has only a few hundred orders and the months after August 2018 are incomplete: monthly trends should use 2017-01 to 2018-08. Totals, shares and averages use all orders unless the question names a period.
