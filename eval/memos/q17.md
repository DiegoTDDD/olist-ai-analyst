# How many installments do credit card orders use on average?

**Decision:** To understand the payment behavior of customers who use credit cards on the Olist marketplace, we need to calculate the average number of installments they use for their orders.
**Metric:** Average number of credit card installments

## Answer

Credit card orders on the Olist marketplace use an average of 3.55 installments.

## Recommendation

Consider offering more options for credit card installments to cater to customer preferences and potentially increase sales.

## Evidence

```sql
SELECT AVG(installments) AS avg_installments FROM orders WHERE payment_type = 'credit_card'
```

| avg_installments |
|---|
| 3.55 |

## Checks

- SQL passed the read-only checks and ran (1 rows, 1 attempt).
- All numbers in this memo were found in the query result.

## Caveats

This average is based on the data from September 2016 to October 2018 and may not reflect current trends. Orders run from 2016-09-04 to 2018-10-17. 2016 has only a few hundred orders and the months after August 2018 are incomplete: monthly trends should use 2017-01 to 2018-08. Totals, shares and averages use all orders unless the question names a period.
