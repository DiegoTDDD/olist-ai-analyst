# What share of item revenue comes from sellers in São Paulo state (SP)?

**Decision:** To understand the contribution of sellers in São Paulo state (SP) to the overall item revenue of the Olist marketplace.
**Metric:** Share of item revenue from SP sellers

## Answer

Sellers in São Paulo state (SP) generate 65.50% of the item revenue on the Olist marketplace.

## Recommendation

Focus on supporting and expanding seller networks in SP to further increase revenue.

## Evidence

```sql
SELECT ROUND(SUM(CASE WHEN seller_state = 'SP' THEN items_value ELSE 0 END) / SUM(items_value), 4) AS sp_revenue_share FROM orders JOIN order_items ON orders.order_id = order_items.order_id
```

| sp_revenue_share |
|---|
| 0.6550 |

## Caveats

This metric does not account for other factors that may influence revenue, such as shipping costs or customer reviews. Orders run from 2016-09-04 to 2018-10-17. 2016 has only a few hundred orders and the months after August 2018 are incomplete: monthly trends should use 2017-01 to 2018-08. Totals, shares and averages use all orders unless the question names a period.
