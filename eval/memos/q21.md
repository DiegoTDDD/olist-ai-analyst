# What share of item revenue comes from sellers in São Paulo state (SP)?

**Decision:** To understand the contribution of São Paulo sellers to the overall revenue of the Olist marketplace.
**Metric:** Share of item revenue from São Paulo sellers

## Answer

São Paulo sellers account for 64.00% of the item revenue on the Olist marketplace.

## Recommendation

Focus on supporting and growing São Paulo sellers to increase overall revenue.

## Evidence

```sql
SELECT ROUND(SUM(CASE WHEN sellers.seller_state = 'SP' THEN order_items.price ELSE 0 END) / SUM(order_items.price), 2) AS sp_revenue_share FROM order_items JOIN sellers ON order_items.seller_id = sellers.seller_id
```

| sp_revenue_share |
|---|
| 0.6400 |

## Caveats

This data does not include the impact of other factors such as shipping costs or customer reviews. Orders run from 2016-09-04 to 2018-10-17. 2016 has only a few hundred orders and the months after August 2018 are incomplete, so trends should use 2017-01 to 2018-08.
