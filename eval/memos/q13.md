# What share of orders was canceled?

**Decision:** To understand the operational efficiency and customer satisfaction by identifying the proportion of orders that were canceled.
**Metric:** Cancellation Rate

## Answer

5.9% of orders were canceled.

## Recommendation

Monitor the reasons for cancellations to improve customer service and reduce the cancellation rate.

## Evidence

```sql
SELECT ROUND(SUM(CASE WHEN status = 'canceled' THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) / COUNT(order_id), 4) AS cancellation_rate FROM orders WHERE purchase_month BETWEEN '2017-01-01' AND '2018-08-01'
```

| 0.0059 |

## Caveats

The data does not show the reasons for cancellations. Orders run from 2016-09-04 to 2018-10-17. 2016 has only a few hundred orders and the months after August 2018 are incomplete: monthly trends should use 2017-01 to 2018-08. Totals, shares and averages use all orders unless the question names a period.
