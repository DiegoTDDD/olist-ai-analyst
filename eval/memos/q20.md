# How did the monthly late delivery rate evolve between January 2017 and August 2018?

**Decision:** To understand the trend of late deliveries over time and identify any seasonal patterns or improvements in delivery performance.
**Metric:** Late delivery rate

## Answer

The late delivery rate increased from 2.93% in January 2017 to 6.19% in August 2018, a 111.3% increase over 20 months.

## Recommendation

Investigate the reasons for the increase in late deliveries, such as seasonal factors or supply chain issues, and implement strategies to improve delivery performance.

## Trend

late_delivery_rate went from 0.0293 in 2017-01 to 0.0619 in 2018-08 (+111.3%) over 20 full months; the trend is rising, +107.9% along a fitted line.

## Evidence

```sql
WITH monthly_late AS ( SELECT purchase_month, AVG(is_late::INTEGER) AS late_rate FROM orders WHERE purchase_month BETWEEN '2017-01-01' AND '2018-08-01' GROUP BY purchase_month ) SELECT purchase_month, ROUND(late_rate, 4) AS late_delivery_rate FROM monthly_late ORDER BY purchase_month
```

## Caveats

The data does not show the underlying causes of the late deliveries or the effectiveness of any interventions. Orders run from 2016-09-04 to 2018-10-17. 2016 has only a few hundred orders and the months after August 2018 are incomplete: monthly trends should use 2017-01 to 2018-08. Totals, shares and averages use all orders unless the question names a period.
