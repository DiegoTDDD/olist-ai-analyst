# What share of orders shipped to customers in the seller's own state? Count orders whose first item's seller is in the customer's state.

**Decision:** To understand the efficiency and customer satisfaction of orders shipped to the seller's own state, which can indicate local supply chain advantages.
**Metric:** Proportion of orders shipped to the seller's own state

## Answer

36.18% of orders were shipped to the seller's own state.

## Recommendation

Invest in local logistics to improve the efficiency of these orders and enhance customer satisfaction.

## Evidence

```sql
SELECT ROUND(SUM(CASE WHEN o.customer_state = s.seller_state THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) / COUNT(*), 4) AS prop_shipped_to_seller_state FROM orders o JOIN order_items oi ON o.order_id = oi.order_id JOIN sellers s ON oi.seller_id = s.seller_id
```

| prop_shipped_to_seller_state |
|---|
| 0.3618 |

## Caveats

This metric does not account for orders where the first item's seller is not in the customer's state. Orders run from 2016-09-04 to 2018-10-17. 2016 has only a few hundred orders and the months after August 2018 are incomplete: monthly trends should use 2017-01 to 2018-08. Totals, shares and averages use all orders unless the question names a period.
