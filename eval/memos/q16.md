# What is the average review score of orders delivered more than 10 days after the promised date?

**Decision:** To understand the customer satisfaction for orders that were delayed by more than 10 days, which could help in identifying potential issues with delivery processes.
**Metric:** Average review score of delayed orders

## Answer

The average review score for orders delivered more than 10 days after the promised date is 1.71.

## Recommendation

Investigate the reasons behind the delayed deliveries to improve customer satisfaction and reduce negative reviews.

## Evidence

```sql
SELECT AVG(review_score) AS avg_review_score FROM orders WHERE is_late = TRUE AND days_late > 10
```

| avg_review_score |
|---|
| 1.71 |

## Caveats

This metric does not account for orders that were delivered on time or early. Orders run from 2016-09-04 to 2018-10-17. 2016 has only a few hundred orders and the months after August 2018 are incomplete: monthly trends should use 2017-01 to 2018-08. Totals, shares and averages use all orders unless the question names a period.
