# How many orders were delivered to customers?

**Decision:** To understand the operational efficiency of the delivery process.
**Metric:** Number of delivered orders

## Answer

96,203 orders were delivered to customers between January 2017 and August 2018.

## Recommendation

Monitor delivery times and identify any trends or issues that may be affecting delivery efficiency.

## Evidence

```sql
SELECT COUNT(order_id) AS delivered_orders FROM orders WHERE delivered_at IS NOT NULL AND purchase_month BETWEEN '2017-01-01' AND '2018-08-01'
```

| delivered_orders |
|---|
| 96203 |

## Checks

- SQL passed the read-only checks and ran (1 rows, 1 attempt).
- **Unverified:** these numbers are not in the query result: 2017, 2018.

## Caveats

This data does not include orders that were not delivered or were canceled. Orders run from 2016-09-04 to 2018-10-17. 2016 has only a few hundred orders and the months after August 2018 are incomplete, so trends should use 2017-01 to 2018-08.
