# How many orders were delivered to customers?

**Decision:** To understand the operational efficiency of the delivery process and customer satisfaction.
**Metric:** Number of delivered orders

## Answer

96,478 orders were delivered to customers.

## Recommendation

Continue to monitor delivery times and customer reviews to ensure high levels of customer satisfaction.

## Evidence

```sql
SELECT COUNT(order_id) AS delivered_orders FROM orders WHERE status = 'delivered'
```

| delivered_orders |
|---|
| 96478 |

## Checks

- SQL passed the read-only checks and ran (1 rows, 1 attempt).
- All numbers in this memo were found in the query result.

## Caveats

This count does not include orders that were not delivered or for which the delivery status is unknown. Orders run from 2016-09-04 to 2018-10-17. 2016 has only a few hundred orders and the months after August 2018 are incomplete: monthly trends should use 2017-01 to 2018-08. Totals, shares and averages use all orders unless the question names a period.
