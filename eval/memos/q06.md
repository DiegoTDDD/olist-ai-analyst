# How did the number of orders per month change from January 2017 to August 2018?

**Decision:** To understand the growth or decline in sales over time, which can help in planning inventory, staffing, and marketing strategies.
**Metric:** Number of orders per month

## Answer

The number of orders per month increased from 800 in January 2017 to 6,512 in August 2018, a rise of 714.0% over 20 months.

## Recommendation

Continue to invest in marketing and inventory to capitalize on this growth trend.

## Trend

order_count went from 800 in 2017-01 to 6,512 in 2018-08 (+714.0%) over 20 full months; the trend is rising, +285.0% along a fitted line.

## Evidence

```sql
SELECT purchase_month, COUNT(order_id) AS order_count FROM orders WHERE purchase_month BETWEEN '2017-01-01' AND '2018-08-01' GROUP BY purchase_month ORDER BY purchase_month
```

## Caveats

The trend is based on data up to August 2018, and future trends may vary. Orders run from 2016-09-04 to 2018-10-17. 2016 has only a few hundred orders and the months after August 2018 are incomplete: monthly trends should use 2017-01 to 2018-08. Totals, shares and averages use all orders unless the question names a period.
