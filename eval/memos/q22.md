# Which 3 product categories have the lowest average review score, among categories with at least 1000 reviewed orders? Use the category of each order's first item and leave out the 'unknown' category.

**Decision:** Identify product categories with the lowest average review scores to understand areas for improvement in product quality or customer satisfaction.
**Metric:** Average review score

## Answer

The three product categories with the lowest average review scores are office_furniture with 3.49, bed_bath_table with 3.90, and furniture_decor with 3.91. These categories have an average review score below 4, indicating areas for improvement in product quality or customer satisfaction.

## Recommendation

Investigate the reasons for the lower review scores in office_furniture and consider quality improvements or marketing strategies to enhance customer satisfaction in these categories.

## Evidence

```sql
SELECT category, AVG(review_score) AS avg_review_score FROM orders JOIN order_items ON orders.order_id = order_items.order_id WHERE category != 'unknown' GROUP BY category HAVING COUNT(order_items.order_id) >= 1000 ORDER BY avg_review_score ASC LIMIT 3
```

| category | avg_review_score |
|---|---|
| office_furniture | 3.49 |
| bed_bath_table | 3.90 |
| furniture_decor | 3.91 |
