# For which 5 product categories with at least 500 items sold is freight the largest share of item price, measured as total freight divided by total item price?

**Decision:** Identify product categories with high freight costs to optimize logistics and potentially reduce shipping fees.
**Metric:** Freight share of item price

## Answer

The five product categories with the largest freight share of item price are electronics, furniture_living_room, food, office_furniture, and furniture_decor, each with a share of 29.00%, 26.00%, 25.00%, 25.00%, and 24.00%, respectively.

## Recommendation

Focus on optimizing logistics for electronics, furniture_living_room, and food categories to reduce shipping costs.

## Evidence

```sql
SELECT category, ROUND(SUM(freight_value) / SUM(price), 2) AS freight_share FROM order_items WHERE category != 'unknown' GROUP BY category HAVING COUNT(*) >= 500 ORDER BY freight_share DESC LIMIT 5
```

## Caveats

The data does not show the total number of items sold in each category, so it cannot be determined if these categories have sold at least 500 items. Orders run from 2016-09-04 to 2018-10-17. 2016 has only a few hundred orders and the months after August 2018 are incomplete: monthly trends should use 2017-01 to 2018-08. Totals, shares and averages use all orders unless the question names a period.
