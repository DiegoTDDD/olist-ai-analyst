# For which 5 categories with at least 500 items sold is freight the largest share of the item price?

**Decision:** Identify categories with high freight costs to optimize logistics and potentially reduce shipping fees.
**Metric:** Freight share of item price

## Answer

The five categories with the largest share of freight costs are electronics (68.35%), telephony (50.59%), housewares (40.27%), home appliances (39.68%), and consoles_games (37.72%).

## Recommendation

Focus on optimizing logistics for electronics and telephony to reduce shipping costs. Consider offering discounts on freight for these categories to increase sales.

## Evidence

```sql
SELECT category, AVG(freight_value / price) AS avg_freight_share FROM order_items WHERE category != 'unknown' GROUP BY category HAVING COUNT(*) >= 500 ORDER BY avg_freight_share DESC LIMIT 5
```
