# Which 5 sellers had the most item revenue, and from which state are they?

**Decision:** Identify the top 5 sellers with the highest revenue to focus marketing efforts and resource allocation.
**Metric:** Total item revenue

## Answer

The top 5 sellers with the highest revenue are from São Paulo (SP), with a total revenue of 1,148,658.5 BRL. The highest revenue comes from seller 4869f7a5dfa277a7dca6462dcf3b52b2 with 229,472.6 BRL.

## Recommendation

Focus marketing efforts on sellers from São Paulo, as they generate the most revenue. Consider offering promotions or incentives to these sellers to further increase their sales.

## Evidence

```sql
SELECT seller_id, seller_state, SUM(price) AS total_revenue FROM orders JOIN order_items ON orders.order_id = order_items.order_id GROUP BY seller_id, seller_state ORDER BY total_revenue DESC LIMIT 5
```
