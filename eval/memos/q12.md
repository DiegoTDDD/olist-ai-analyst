# Which 5 sellers had the most item revenue, and from which state are they?

**Decision:** Identify top-performing sellers based on item revenue to focus marketing efforts and resource allocation.
**Metric:** Total item revenue

## Answer

The top 5 sellers by item revenue are from São Paulo (SP), with revenues of R$229,472.60, R$200,472.90, R$194,042.00, R$187,923.90, and R$222,776.00 respectively.

## Recommendation

Focus marketing efforts on these top-performing sellers in São Paulo to maximize revenue.

## Evidence

```sql
SELECT seller_id, seller_state, SUM(price) AS total_revenue FROM orders JOIN order_items ON orders.order_id = order_items.order_id GROUP BY seller_id, seller_state ORDER BY total_revenue DESC LIMIT 5
```
