# What was the total payment value of all orders placed in 2017?

**Decision:** To understand the financial performance of the e-commerce platform in 2017.
**Metric:** Total payment value

## Answer

The total payment value of all orders placed in 2017 was 7,249,746.7 BRL.

## Recommendation

Monitor the payment trends to ensure financial health and customer satisfaction.

## Evidence

```sql
SELECT SUM(payment_value) AS total_payment_value FROM orders WHERE purchase_month BETWEEN '2017-01-01' AND '2017-12-01'
```

| total_payment_value |
|---|
| 7,249,746.7 |

## Checks

- SQL passed the read-only checks and ran (1 rows, 1 attempt).
- All numbers in this memo were found in the query result.

## Caveats

This data does not include orders from 2018, which may affect the overall financial performance. Orders run from 2016-09-04 to 2018-10-17. 2016 has only a few hundred orders and the months after August 2018 are incomplete: monthly trends should use 2017-01 to 2018-08. Totals, shares and averages use all orders unless the question names a period.
