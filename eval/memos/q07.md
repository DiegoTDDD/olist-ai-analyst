# What share of orders is paid mainly with each payment type (credit_card, boleto, voucher, debit_card)?

**Decision:** To understand the payment preferences of customers on the Olist marketplace, which payment types are most commonly used, and how they compare to each other.
**Metric:** Payment type share

## Answer

Most orders are paid with credit card (78%), followed by boleto (18%). Vouchers and debit cards are less common, with 2% and 1% of orders respectively. A small number of orders have no payment type defined.

## Recommendation

Continue to focus on credit card payments, as they account for over 78% of all orders. Consider offering more payment options to attract more customers, especially those who prefer boleto payments.

## Evidence

```sql
SELECT payment_type, ROUND(SUM(payment_value) / (SELECT SUM(payment_value) FROM orders), 2) AS payment_type_share FROM orders GROUP BY payment_type ORDER BY payment_type_share DESC LIMIT 10
```

| payment_type | payment_type_share |
|---|---|
| credit_card | 0.7800 |
| boleto | 0.1800 |
| voucher | 0.0200 |
| debit_card | 0.0100 |
| None | 0 |
| not_defined | 0 |

## Caveats

The data does not show the trend over time or the distribution of payment types across different regions. Orders run from 2016-09-04 to 2018-10-17. 2016 has only a few hundred orders and the months after August 2018 are incomplete, so trends should use 2017-01 to 2018-08.
