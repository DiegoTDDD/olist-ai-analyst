# What share of orders is paid mainly with each payment type (credit_card, boleto, voucher, debit_card)?

**Decision:** To understand the payment preferences of customers on the Olist marketplace, which payment types are most commonly used, and how they compare to each other.
**Metric:** Payment type share

## Answer

Credit card is the most commonly used payment method, accounting for 78.35% of all payments. Boleto follows with 17.92%, while voucher and debit card have smaller shares of 2.37% and 1.36%, respectively. None and not_defined payment types together make up less than 1% of all payments.

## Recommendation

Continue to focus on credit card processing to maximize sales. Consider offering more convenience options like boleto for customers who prefer it.

## Evidence

```sql
SELECT payment_type, ROUND(SUM(payment_value) / (SELECT SUM(payment_value) FROM orders), 4) AS payment_type_share FROM orders GROUP BY payment_type ORDER BY payment_type_share DESC
```

| payment_type | payment_type_share |
|---|---|
| credit_card | 0.7835 |
| boleto | 0.1792 |
| voucher | 0.0237 |
| debit_card | 0.0136 |
| None | 0 |
| not_defined | 0 |

## Caveats

The data does not provide information on the reasons behind the low usage of voucher and debit card payments. Orders run from 2016-09-04 to 2018-10-17. 2016 has only a few hundred orders and the months after August 2018 are incomplete: monthly trends should use 2017-01 to 2018-08. Totals, shares and averages use all orders unless the question names a period.
