# What is the average number of days from purchase to delivery for delivered orders?

**Decision:** To understand the efficiency of the delivery process and identify areas for improvement.
**Metric:** Average delivery days

## Answer

The average number of days from purchase to delivery for delivered orders is 12.50 days.

## Recommendation

Monitor delivery times closely and identify any delays that may be affecting the delivery process.

## Evidence

```sql
SELECT AVG(delivery_days) AS avg_delivery_days FROM orders WHERE status = 'delivered'
```

| avg_delivery_days |
|---|
| 12.50 |

## Checks

- SQL passed the read-only checks and ran (1 rows, 1 attempt).
- All numbers in this memo were found in the query result.

## Caveats

This metric does not account for orders that have not yet been delivered. Orders run from 2016-09-04 to 2018-10-17. 2016 has only a few hundred orders and the months after August 2018 are incomplete: monthly trends should use 2017-01 to 2018-08. Totals, shares and averages use all orders unless the question names a period.
