# What is the average amount paid per delivered order?

**Decision:** To understand the financial performance of delivered orders and identify potential areas for improvement in the payment process.
**Metric:** Average payment value per delivered order

## Answer

The average amount paid per delivered order is R$159.85.

## Recommendation

Monitor the payment process to ensure timely and accurate payments, as this could impact the overall financial health of the business.

## Evidence

```sql
SELECT AVG(payment_value) AS avg_payment_per_delivered_order FROM orders WHERE status = 'delivered'
```

| avg_payment_per_delivered_order |
|---|
| 159.85 |

## Checks

- SQL passed the read-only checks and ran (1 rows, 1 attempt).
- All numbers in this memo were found in the query result.

## Caveats

This metric does not account for orders that were not delivered or canceled. Orders run from 2016-09-04 to 2018-10-17. 2016 has only a few hundred orders and the months after August 2018 are incomplete: monthly trends should use 2017-01 to 2018-08. Totals, shares and averages use all orders unless the question names a period.
