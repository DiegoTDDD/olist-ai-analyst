# Which 5 customer states have the highest late delivery rate, among states with at least 1000 delivered orders?

**Decision:** Identify customer states with high late delivery rates to focus on improving delivery performance in those areas.
**Metric:** Late delivery rate

## Answer

The states with the highest late delivery rates are SP (1820.0%) and RJ (1495.0%).

## Recommendation

Focus on improving delivery performance in SP and RJ to reduce late deliveries.

## Evidence

```sql
WITH late_deliveries AS ( SELECT customer_state, COUNT(*) AS late_count FROM orders WHERE status = 'delivered' AND is_late = TRUE GROUP BY customer_state HAVING COUNT(*) >= 1000 ) SELECT orders.customer_state, ROUND(SUM(late_count) / COUNT(*)::FLOAT, 4) AS late_rate FROM orders JOIN late_deliveries ON orders.customer_state = late_deliveries.customer_state WHERE orders.status = 'delivered' GROUP BY orders.customer_state ORDER BY late_rate DESC LIMIT 5
```

| customer_state | late_rate |
|---|---|
| SP | 1,820.0 |
| RJ | 1,495 |

## Caveats

The data does not show the reasons for late deliveries or trends over time. Orders run from 2016-09-04 to 2018-10-17. 2016 has only a few hundred orders and the months after August 2018 are incomplete: monthly trends should use 2017-01 to 2018-08. Totals, shares and averages use all orders unless the question names a period.
