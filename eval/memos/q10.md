# What share of customers placed more than one order?

**Decision:** To understand customer loyalty and retention, we need to know how many customers have made multiple purchases on the platform.
**Metric:** Share of customers with more than one order

## Answer

31.2% of customers placed more than one order on the platform.

## Recommendation

Implement loyalty programs to encourage repeat business and increase customer retention.

## Evidence

```sql
SELECT ROUND(SUM(CASE WHEN order_count > 1 THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) / COUNT(DISTINCT customer_unique_id), 4) AS share_customers_more_than_one_order FROM ( SELECT customer_unique_id, COUNT(order_id) AS order_count FROM orders GROUP BY customer_unique_id ) AS customer_orders
```

## Caveats

The data does not show the frequency or value of repeat orders, only that 31.2% of customers have made multiple purchases. Orders run from 2016-09-04 to 2018-10-17. 2016 has only a few hundred orders and the months after August 2018 are incomplete: monthly trends should use 2017-01 to 2018-08. Totals, shares and averages use all orders unless the question names a period.
