# What share of customers placed more than one order?

**Decision:** To understand customer loyalty and retention, it's important to know how many customers place multiple orders. This can help in identifying repeat customers and potentially in marketing strategies to retain them.
**Metric:** Proportion of customers who placed more than one order

## Answer

31.2% of customers placed more than one order.

## Recommendation

Focus on retaining these repeat customers through targeted marketing strategies.

## Evidence

```sql
SELECT ROUND(SUM(CASE WHEN order_count > 1 THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) / COUNT(DISTINCT customer_unique_id), 4) AS repeat_customer_rate FROM ( SELECT customer_unique_id, COUNT(order_id) AS order_count FROM orders GROUP BY customer_unique_id ) AS customer_orders
```

## Caveats

This data does not show the frequency or value of repeat orders. Orders run from 2016-09-04 to 2018-10-17. 2016 has only a few hundred orders and the months after August 2018 are incomplete, so trends should use 2017-01 to 2018-08.
