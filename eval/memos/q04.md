# Which 5 product categories sold the most in item revenue (price, without freight)?

**Decision:** Identify the most popular product categories based on revenue to focus marketing efforts and inventory management.
**Metric:** Total item revenue (price, without freight)

## Answer

The top 5 product categories by item revenue are health_beauty, watches_gifts, bed_bath_table, sports_leisure, and computers_accessories, with revenues of 1,258,681.3, 1,205,005.7, 1,036,988.7, 988,049.0, and 911,954.3 respectively.

## Recommendation

Focus marketing efforts and inventory management on health_beauty and watches_gifts, as they generate the most revenue.

## Evidence

```sql
SELECT category, SUM(price) AS total_revenue FROM order_items GROUP BY category ORDER BY total_revenue DESC LIMIT 5
```

| category | total_revenue |
|---|---|
| health_beauty | 1,258,681.3 |
| watches_gifts | 1,205,005.7 |
| bed_bath_table | 1,036,988.7 |
| sports_leisure | 988,049.0 |
| computers_accessories | 911,954.3 |

## Checks

- All numbers in this memo were found in the query result.

## Caveats

The data does not show the trend over time or the contribution of each category to the total revenue. Orders run from 2016-09-04 to 2018-10-17. 2016 has only a few hundred orders and the months after August 2018 are incomplete: monthly trends should use 2017-01 to 2018-08. Totals, shares and averages use all orders unless the question names a period.
