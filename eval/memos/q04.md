# Which 5 product categories sold the most in item revenue (price, without freight)?

**Decision:** Identify the most popular product categories based on revenue to focus marketing efforts and inventory management.
**Metric:** Total item revenue (price, without freight)

## Answer

Health and beauty products generated the highest revenue with $1,253,993.90, followed by watches and gifts with $1,201,645.40.

## Recommendation

Focus marketing efforts on health and beauty products and consider expanding the watches and gifts category.

## Evidence

```sql
SELECT category, SUM(price) AS total_revenue FROM order_items WHERE purchase_month BETWEEN '2017-01-01' AND '2018-08-01' GROUP BY category ORDER BY total_revenue DESC LIMIT 5
```

| category | total_revenue |
|---|---|
| health_beauty | 1,253,993.9 |
| watches_gifts | 1,201,645.4 |
| bed_bath_table | 1,036,509.7 |
| sports_leisure | 984,715.3 |
| computers_accessories | 910,555.0 |

## Checks

- SQL passed the read-only checks and ran (5 rows, 1 attempt).
- All numbers in this memo were found in the query result.

## Caveats

The data does not include revenue from other product categories. Orders run from 2016-09-04 to 2018-10-17. 2016 has only a few hundred orders and the months after August 2018 are incomplete, so trends should use 2017-01 to 2018-08.
