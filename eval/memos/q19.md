# What share of delivered orders got a 1-star review?

**Decision:** To understand the customer satisfaction level of delivered orders and identify areas for improvement.
**Metric:** Share of delivered orders with a 1-star review

## Answer

9.69% of delivered orders received a 1-star review.

## Recommendation

Investigate the reasons behind the low reviews and take corrective actions to improve customer satisfaction.

## Evidence

```sql
SELECT ROUND(SUM(CASE WHEN review_score = 1 THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) / COUNT(order_id), 4) AS share_1_star FROM orders WHERE status = 'delivered'
```

| share_1_star |
|---|
| 0.0969 |

## Caveats

This metric does not include orders that were not delivered. Orders run from 2016-09-04 to 2018-10-17. 2016 has only a few hundred orders and the months after August 2018 are incomplete: monthly trends should use 2017-01 to 2018-08. Totals, shares and averages use all orders unless the question names a period.
