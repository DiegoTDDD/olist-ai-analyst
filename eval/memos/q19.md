# Among delivered orders that have a review, what share got a 1-star review?

**Decision:** To understand the customer satisfaction level, specifically the proportion of dissatisfied customers who gave a 1-star review among those who left reviews.
**Metric:** Share of 1-star reviews among delivered orders with reviews

## Answer

Among delivered orders that have a review, 9.76% received a 1-star review.

## Recommendation

Investigate the reasons behind the low customer satisfaction and take appropriate actions to improve the product or service.

## Evidence

```sql
SELECT ROUND(SUM(review_score = 1)::FLOAT / COUNT(order_id), 4) AS share_1_star_reviews FROM orders WHERE status = 'delivered' AND review_score IS NOT NULL
```

| share_1_star_reviews |
|---|
| 0.0976 |

## Caveats

This data does not include orders without reviews, so it may not fully represent the overall customer experience. Orders run from 2016-09-04 to 2018-10-17. 2016 has only a few hundred orders and the months after August 2018 are incomplete: monthly trends should use 2017-01 to 2018-08. Totals, shares and averages use all orders unless the question names a period.
