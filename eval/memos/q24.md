# In which month between January 2017 and August 2018 were the most orders placed, and how many?

**Decision:** Identify the peak month for order volume to optimize inventory and staffing.
**Metric:** Number of orders

## Answer

The most orders were placed in November 2017 with 7,544 orders.

## Recommendation

Increase inventory and staffing in November 2017 to meet the high demand.

## Evidence

```sql
SELECT purchase_month, COUNT(order_id) AS order_count FROM orders WHERE purchase_month BETWEEN '2017-01-01' AND '2018-08-01' GROUP BY purchase_month ORDER BY order_count DESC LIMIT 1
```

| purchase_month | order_count |
|---|---|
| 2017-11-01 | 7544 |

## Checks

- SQL passed the read-only checks and ran (1 rows, 1 attempt).
- All numbers in this memo were found in the query result.

## Caveats

This data does not account for future trends or other seasonal factors. Orders run from 2016-09-04 to 2018-10-17. 2016 has only a few hundred orders and the months after August 2018 are incomplete, so trends should use 2017-01 to 2018-08.
