# What share of delivered orders arrived after the promised date?

**Decision:** To assess the performance of the delivery process and identify areas for improvement.
**Metric:** Share of delivered orders that arrived after the promised date

## Answer

6.77% of delivered orders arrived after the promised date.

## Recommendation

Implement stricter quality control and delivery processes to reduce late deliveries.

## Evidence

```sql
SELECT ROUND(SUM(is_late::INTEGER) / COUNT(order_id), 4) AS late_delivery_rate FROM orders WHERE status = 'delivered'
```

## Caveats

This metric does not account for orders that were not delivered. Orders run from 2016-09-04 to 2018-10-17. 2016 has only a few hundred orders and the months after August 2018 are incomplete: monthly trends should use 2017-01 to 2018-08. Totals, shares and averages use all orders unless the question names a period.
